Companion to Weekly Briefing Issue #35 (4–10 September 2026)

This week California became the first jurisdiction anywhere to license the people who audit AI, signing SB 813 and AB 1405 into law — landing in the same 72 hours as two of the year's hardest EU and Korean deadlines and a $13 billion money-laundering signal from FinCEN.

Top Stories

AI Governance · California California licenses the first AI auditors. Governor Newsom signed SB 813 and AB 1405 on 9 September — the first US statutes to regulate who may assess AI systems. SB 813 creates independent verification organizations; AB 1405 establishes a state registry for AI auditors. Conducting a covered audit without registration becomes a prohibited act from 1 January 2029.
Governor of California — signing release · SB 813 · AB 1405
Cybersecurity · EU EU Cyber Resilience Act — Article 14 reporting goes live. Due 11 Sep Manufacturers of products with digital elements must report actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents on a 24h / 72h / 14-day clock through ENISA's Single Reporting Platform, which launches the same day. Scope reaches products already on the market, plus software components and remote data-processing solutions.
European Commission — CRA reporting guidance
Privacy · South Korea Korea's amended PIPA takes effect. Due 11 Sep The amendment names the CEO as ultimately responsible for data-protection compliance and adds a punitive fine track of up to 10% of total annual turnover for the most serious systemic failures, alongside the existing 3% baseline.
PIPC — Korea Personal Information Protection Commission
Cloud · EU EU Data Act — data-access-by-design attaches to connected products. Due 12 Sep Under Regulation (EU) 2023/2854 Art. 3(1), connected products newly placed on the EU market must be designed so in-use and readily available data are directly accessible to the user by default.
EUR-Lex — Regulation (EU) 2023/2854 (Data Act)
Financial · US FinCEN flags nearly $13B in crypto "scam center" fraud. FIN-2026-Alert005 (3 September) maps roughly $12.7 billion tied to overseas scam-center fraud from BSA filings (Sep 2023–Dec 2025). No new obligation, but examiners will expect the red-flag typologies folded into transaction monitoring and SAR narratives, and 314(b) sharing reassessed.
FinCEN — news release

Enforcement Watch

  • Spain AEPD fines Yoti €950,000 — vendor pulled its Digital ID app from Spanish stores (effective 10 Sep) rather than add a non-biometric path. Resolución PS/00164/2025
  • CNIL fines Hôpital Privé de la Loire €500,000 — GDPR Arts. 32/34, after a 2025 intrusion reached ~524,867 patient records via remote access with no VPN and no MFA. CNIL — sanction
