The Wall Hits This Weekend

Thursday, 30 July 2026 · ~8 min · Actionable Regulatory Intelligence

Ten days ago we flagged an August wall. It is now three days away — and no longer a single wall but a cluster of hard gates across five jurisdictions. This episode walks the deadlines that bind this weekend and the three new laws that just landed, with what to do about each.

Top 5 Stories

  1. EU AI Act Article 50 transparency + GPAI fining powers apply (2 Aug). Interactive systems must disclose AI interaction, generative outputs must be machine-readably marked, and deepfakes labelled. The Commission's power to fine general-purpose AI providers up to €15M or 3% of worldwide turnover switches on the same day. A narrow grace period defers only the Article 50(2) marking duty for pre-market systems to 2 Dec 2026.
    European Commission — Article 50 transparency FAQ
  2. California Delete Act (DROP) deletion-processing duty binds (1 Aug). Every registered California data broker must access DROP at least every 45 days, match and delete relevant personal information including inferences, cascade the instruction to service providers, and report each request's status within the window. Non-compliance runs at $200 per day, per unhandled deletion request. 300,000+ Californians enrolled; 600+ brokers registered.
    CPPA — DROP for Data Brokers
  3. Germany BSI NIS2 registration expectation closes (31 Jul). Following a BSI letter to industry, in-scope entities are expected to have registered by 31 July, after which non-registration becomes an enforcement priority. §65 BSIG fines reach €500,000 plus management personal liability.
    BSI — NIS-2 Registrierungspflicht
  4. Ireland enacts its AI Act implementing law. The Regulation of Artificial Intelligence Act 2026 (Act No. 31 of 2026), signed 21 July, establishes the AI Office of Ireland nine days before the AI Act applies and slots sectoral regulators into the enforcement architecture.
    Oireachtas — Regulation of Artificial Intelligence Bill 2026
  5. FedRAMP 20x Class A pipeline opens (3 Aug). With FedRAMP Ready retired to Legacy on 28 July, the Class A Program Certification path — no agency sponsor required, assessed against 25 mandatory rules with a current SOC 2 Type II as the evidentiary spine — is the first authorization on-ramp under CR26. Class B/C pipelines open 31 August.
    FedRAMP.gov
