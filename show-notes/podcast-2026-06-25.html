EU AI Act signoff nears; June 30 deadline wall

June 25, 2026 · ~8 min listen

The Council of the EU is expected to formally adopt the AI Act Digital Omnibus on June 29 — the last step before the deferred high-risk dates are fixed in the Official Journal. But there is no breathing room: the first NIS2 compliance audits and FedRAMP's CR26 rewrite both land June 30, and a dense July 1 wall switches on new rules across China, Vietnam, and two US states.

Top 5 Stories

  1. EU AI Act delay nears finality. The Council of the EU is expected to adopt the Digital Omnibus on AI on June 29, fixing the high-risk deferral to December 2, 2027 (Annex III) and August 2, 2028 (Annex I) and clearing the way for Official Journal publication in July. Article 50 transparency duties are carved out and still apply August 2, 2026.
    EC Digital Strategy · Council press release
  2. Twin deadlines land June 30. The first NIS2 compliance audits fall due in transposed member states (the controlling date is national, not EU-wide) the same day FedRAMP's CR26 rewrite finalizes, retiring impact levels for Certification Classes A–D.
    NIS2 Directive · FedRAMP CR26
  3. A July 1 wall spans three continents. China's Order 837 outbound-investment regime, its first energy-sector data rules, and two PIPL standards (GB/T 46901 portability, GB/T 46903 audits); Vietnam's unified Cybersecurity Law 2025 (No. 116/2025/QH15); and Connecticut SB 1295 and Tennessee SB 1580 all take effect at once.
    CT PA 25-113 · TN SB 1580 · GB/T standards
  4. Court cracks the country-of-origin shield. On June 16 the Court of Justice of the EU ruled (Joined Cases C-188/24 and C-190/24) that a member state may require age verification of pornographic services established in other EU countries — narrowing a core single-market protection.
    CJEU press release
  5. Illinois leads on frontier AI. Illinois has sent the nation's first frontier-model audit bill to the governor's desk, pushing third-party audits of the largest AI systems toward law.
    Related: White House June-2 AI EO
