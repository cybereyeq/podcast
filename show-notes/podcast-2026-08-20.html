Top Stories

1. China Order No. 24 in force today. The Network Data Security Risk Assessment Measures (CAC/MIIT/MPS, issued 18 Jun 2026) take effect 20 Aug 2026. Handlers of "important data" owe a documented annual risk assessment, filed with their sector regulator within 20 working days and retained 3 years. Source: CAC Order No. 24.
2. EU Cyber Resilience Act Article 14 reporting begins 11 Sep. Manufacturers of products with digital elements must report actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents via ENISA's Single Reporting Platform: early warning in 24h, full notification in 72h, final report within 14 days. Sources: EC CRA reporting · ENISA SRP.
3. South Korea PIPA overhaul takes effect 11 Sep. A punitive fine track reaching 10% of total annual turnover (for intentional/grossly-negligent repeat conduct, breaches affecting 10M+ individuals, or corrective-order non-compliance); the business owner/representative is named ultimately responsible; CPO appointments at large-scale controllers need board approval and PIPC reporting. Source: PIPC (Korea).
4. California's first data-broker fines under both the CCPA and the Delete Act. LocateSmarter LLC — $116,490 (failure to register by 31 Jan 2026; requiring last-4 SSN before opt-out); Cybba, Inc. — $52,400. Source: CalPrivacy.
5. AB 1651 on California's Governor's desk. Presented 10 Aug; the 12-day gubernatorial clock expires on or about 22 Aug 2026. From 1 Jan 2028 it would require the State Bar to disclose AI-generated content in the bar exam and study materials — and human revision does not extinguish the duty. Source: Assembly Daily Summary (10 Aug 2026), Senate Judiciary analysis.
