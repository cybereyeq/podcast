CyberEyeQ Weekly — Episode 36

The 11 September Wall: CRA and Korea · Thursday, 3 September 2026 · ~7 min

Two of the year's most consequential regimes switch on the same day — 11 September, eight days out: the EU Cyber Resilience Act's 24-hour vulnerability reporting, and South Korea's rewritten PIPA with CEO accountability and fines up to 10% of global turnover. Here's what hits, what's due, and what to do first.

Top Stories

1. EU CRA reporting goes live 11 Sep
Manufacturers of products with digital elements must report actively exploited vulnerabilities to ENISA — early warning within 24h, technical notification within 72h, final report within 14 days. Covers products already on the market.
EC — CRA reporting obligations · ENISA Single Reporting Platform
2. Korea's amended PIPA — 10% fines + CEO liability 11 Sep
Signed 10 Mar 2026; adds a higher penalty tier (up to 10% of turnover for severe/repeat violations or breaches affecting 10M+ people) atop the 3% baseline, plus personal supervisory liability for the CEO.
3. Federal Reserve AML/CFT program NPRM — comment closes 8 Sep
Docket R-1835 requires supervised banks to maintain effective, risk-based AML/CFT programs. File comments or align your BSA program to the standard.
4. China CAC — Large Personal Information Processor draft 7 Sep
The consolidated draft closes for public comment 7 September.
5. CalPrivacy fines data broker SalesIntel
$36,400 for missing the 2025 Data Broker Registry deadline, plus an order to join the Data Removal (DROP) platform. Enforcement Advisory 2026-01 warns of $200/day for inaccurate annual registrations.
6. CISA adds seven exploited CVEs to KEV 2 Sep
Seven actively exploited CVEs added, starting risk-based federal remediation clocks under BOD 26-04.

Compliance Action Items

  • By 7 Sep — Submit comments on China's CAC Large PI Processor draft if in scope.
  • By 8 Sep — File comments on Fed AML/CFT NPRM (R-1835), or begin aligning your BSA program.
  • By 11 Sep — Stand up CRA Article 14 reporting: name the filer, test ENISA SRP access, pre-draft 24h/72h templates.
  • By 11 Sep — Confirm in writing who holds CEO-level PIPA accountability; document privacy-program investment.
  • Now — Check the seven new CISA KEV entries against your estate and prioritize remediation.
