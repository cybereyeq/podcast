EU delays its AI rules as a July compliance wall builds worldwide

June 18, 2026 · ~8 min listen · Hosts: Alex & Sarah

The European Parliament voted to postpone the AI Act's high-risk obligations to 2027–2028 — but the August 2 transparency duties stand. Meanwhile the UK moved to bar under-16s from social media, and more than a dozen compliance obligations land between June 18 and July 18, from today's Basel III comment close to MiCA's crypto hard stop and two new China privacy standards on July 1.

Top Stories

  1. EU Parliament delays the AI Act's high-risk rules. The Digital Omnibus passed 423–57 (174 abstentions) on June 16. Stand-alone high-risk systems (Annex III) slip to Dec 2, 2027; embedded AI (Annex I) to Aug 2, 2028; watermarking/labelling to Dec 2, 2026. Article 50 transparency duties are carved out and still apply Aug 2, 2026. A new EU-wide ban on AI “nudifier” apps takes effect Dec 2, 2026. Council adoption and Official Journal publication are still pending. European Parliament · EC Code of Practice
  2. UK moves to ban under-16s from social media. A bill is expected before Christmas, with default-off livestreaming/stranger contact and a minimum age of 18 for AI “companion” chatbots, layered onto the existing Online Safety Act regime.
  3. A wall of July 1 deadlines. MiCA's crypto transition ends, the EU ESG Ratings Regulation applies, three US state age laws plus Connecticut SB 1295 and Tennessee SB 1580 AI rules switch on, and two China PIPL standards (GB/T 46903 audits, GB/T 46901 data portability) take effect.
  4. Basel III “Endgame” comments close today. The Fed/OCC/FDIC re-proposal would cut common-equity-tier-1 requirements by roughly $87.7bn and poses 200+ questions. Comment window closes June 18. Federal Reserve
  5. HIPAA ransomware settlements hit $1.165M. HHS OCR resolved four Security Rule cases (April 23) tied to ransomware exposing the ePHI of ~427,000 people — its 11th and 12th Risk Analysis Initiative actions. The FTC also finalized its Illuminate Education order over a breach affecting ~10M students. HHS OCR · FTC
