Top Stories This Week

CriticalAI Governance · EU

EU AI Act Omnibus: Deadlines Split, GPAI Obligations Unchanged at Aug 2

The May 7 political agreement shifts Annex III high-risk AI obligations to December 2, 2027 and Annex I regulated-product AI to August 2, 2028 — but GPAI and Article 50 transparency obligations remain fixed at August 2, 2026. Treating the Omnibus as a blanket pause is a compliance risk. Article 50 transparency guidelines consultation closes June 3.

Critical · Effective TodayHealthcare · EU

EUDAMED Now Mandatory — New Devices Require SRN from May 28

The EU medical device database (EUDAMED) became mandatory today. Any new device placed on the EU market requires a live Single Registration Number (SRN) and UDI module entry. No grace period for new devices. Legacy device registration deadline: November 28, 2026.

→ EUDAMED portal
CriticalCybersecurity · EU

NIS2 First Audit Deadline — 33 Days to June 30

The EU's first mandatory NIS2 audit cycle closes June 30. Essential entities face fines up to €10M or 2% of global turnover. Germany's BSI issued 47 formal notices in Q4 2025 alone. Classify your entity, verify 24-hour incident reporting, and register with your national authority now.

→ ENISA NIS2 resources
HighCloud Security · US DoD

CMMC Phase 2: Only 1% Certified, 166 Days to November 10

Just 1,042 of 76,598 DoD contractors have completed CMMC Level 2 certification. C3PAO assessment backlogs run 6-12 months, meaning most organizations that haven't started cannot meet the November 10, 2026 mandatory deadline. Contact a C3PAO this week.

→ DoD CMMC program
AI Governance · Global

Colorado Scraps AI Law; China Confirms Unified AI Statute

Colorado Governor Polis signed SB 26-189 (May 14), replacing SB 24-205's risk-management framework with a disclosure-only ADM regime effective January 1, 2027. Meanwhile, China's State Council confirmed a comprehensive AI law is in development. China's Anthropomorphic AI Interim Measures take effect July 15 (51 days).
