CyberEyeQ Weekly Podcast — Episode 38

Thursday, 17 September 2026 · Actionable Regulatory Intelligence · Covering 11–17 September 2026

This week Brussels turned a State-of-the-Union soundbite into a formal bill — the EU KIDS Act, the first EU measure to pull AI chatbots and companion apps into child-safety law — while a wall of hard deadlines lands: FedRAMP's machine-readable package mandate, NIS2 going live in Austria and Poland, and China's expanded police cyber-inspection powers.

Top 5 Stories

  1. EU tables the KIDS Act. The Commission's proposal would bar under-15s from autonomously creating social and video-sharing accounts and, for the first time at EU level, reaches AI chatbots and companion apps. Still only a proposal — the age-15 floor may move toward the Parliament's age-16 resolution. European Commission — Proposal for the EU KIDS Act
  2. FedRAMP OSCAL mandate hits 30 September. Every Rev5 cloud provider — not just 20x pilot participants — must submit new authorization packages in machine-readable (OSCAL) form; a grace period to 30 Sep 2027 covers converting existing authorizations. FedRAMP — RFC-0024
  3. NIS2 deadlines land in weeks. Austria's NISG 2026 enters full force 1 October; Poland's essential/important-entity registration closes 3 October; Italy's basic-security milestone follows 31 October. Obligations attach through each Member State's transposition law, so dates differ by country. European Commission — NIS2 transposition status
  4. China widens police cyber checks. MPS Order No. 176 replaces the 2018 Order No. 151 and, from 1 October, authorises online patrol, remote vulnerability-probing and penetration-testing of non-critical (non-CII) systems. Ministry of Public Security — Order No. 176
  5. Ireland opens first Online Safety Code probe — into X. Coimisiún na Meán is investigating X's age-assurance measures; a breach can draw up to 10% of relevant annual turnover or €20 million, whichever is greater. Coimisiún na Meán — investigation commenced

Compliance Action Items

  • By 30 Sep: Prepare any new or in-flight FedRAMP Rev5 package in OSCAL, and schedule conversion of existing authorizations before the next annual assessment.
  • By 1–3 Oct: Complete Austrian NISG 2026 obligations (1 Oct), file the Polish essential/important-entity registration (3 Oct), and confirm Italian measures ahead of 31 October.
  • By 1 Oct: Confirm which China-based systems fall within PSB inspection scope and finalise inspection-readiness — access logging, vulnerability remediation, content controls — for MPS Order No. 176.
  • Ongoing (financial institutions): Fold the FinCEN scam-center red-flag typologies (Alert FIN-2026-Alert005) into transaction monitoring and SAR narratives; consider Section 314(b) sharing.
