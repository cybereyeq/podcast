🔍 Top Stories This Week

CRITICAL · EU · Healthcare

EUDAMED Is Now Mandatory

The EU medical device database (EUDAMED) became mandatory on May 28. Any new medical device placed on the EU market requires an active Single Registration Number (SRN). Legacy device deadline: November 28, 2026.

CRITICAL · EU · Cybersecurity

NIS2 First Compliance Audit — June 30 (33 Days)

The EU's first mandatory NIS2 audit cycle closes June 30. Essential entities face fines up to €10M or 2% of global turnover; important entities up to €7M or 1.4%. Germany, France, and the Netherlands are in active enforcement mode. Germany BSI issued 47 formal notices in Q4 2025.

CRITICAL · US · Cloud Security

CMMC Phase 2 — 166 Days, 1% Certified

Only 1,042 of 76,598 (~1.4%) required DoD contractors have completed CMMC Level 2 certification. C3PAO assessment backlogs are 6–12 months. Mandatory assessments begin November 10, 2026.

HIGH · EU · AI Governance

EU AI Act Omnibus — What Actually Changed

The May 7 political agreement pushed Annex III high-risk AI to December 2, 2027 and Annex I systems to August 2, 2028. Not moved: GPAI obligations and Article 50 transparency requirements remain at August 2, 2026. Article 50 consultation closed June 3.

INFO · US / China · AI Governance

Colorado Scraps AI Law; China Confirms Comprehensive Statute

Colorado Governor Polis signed SB 26-189 (May 14), replacing the SB 24-205 risk-management framework with a disclosure-only ADM regime effective January 1, 2027. China's State Council confirmed drafting a unified AI law to consolidate algorithm, generative AI, and anthropomorphic AI rules. China's Anthropomorphic AI Interim Measures take effect July 15.

HIGH · UK · Privacy

UK DUAA Complaint-Handling — June 19 (22 Days)

The Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 requires all UK data controllers to have an operational complaints procedure by June 19. Requirements: electronic submission, 30-day acknowledgement, ICO escalation information. No grace period.

✅ Action Items This Week

1. Verify EUDAMED SRN registration (IMMEDIATE)

If you manufacture, distribute, or represent medical devices in the EU, confirm your SRN is live and new device records are in the UDI module. Mandatory from May 28.

2. Start NIS2 gap assessment (33 days to June 30)

Confirm entity classification (essential vs. important), validate ≤24-hour incident reporting workflows, and register with your national competent authority.

3. Contact a C3PAO immediately (166 days to November 10)

DoD contractors with CUI in cloud environments: schedule a readiness assessment now. 6–12 month backlogs mean you're already at risk if you haven't started.

4. Re-scope Colorado AI compliance (effective January 1, 2027)

If you had a program for SB 24-205, pause it. Restart scoping from the new "Automated Decision-Making Technology" definition under SB 26-189.

5. Publish UK DUAA complaints procedure (22 days to June 19)

All UK data controllers must have an operational, published complaints procedure with electronic submission and 30-day acknowledgement. No grace period.
